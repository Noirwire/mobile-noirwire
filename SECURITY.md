# Security

NoirWire holds people's money. If you find a way to take it, lock it, or learn who owns what, please tell us privately before telling anyone else.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **ph1l1ph@proton.me**.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker gains. A proof of concept helps. Please do not open a public issue, and do not test against wallets or funds that are not yours.

You will get an acknowledgement, and we will keep you informed while we work on a fix. We will credit you when the fix ships unless you would rather we did not.

## What matters most

- Anything that exposes a recovery phrase, a private key or a wallet password, or moves them off the device.
- Anything that signs or sends a transaction the person did not review and confirm.
- Anything that reveals a portfolio's address, or links portfolios to each other or to their owner, through routes, logs, network requests or stored data.
- Anything that lets the app run with weakened randomness or encryption.

## Out of scope

- A device that is already compromised (rooted, jailbroken or running malware).
- Vulnerabilities in third-party services, which should go to their owners.
